Factory Flow Gateway keeps a log of collector activity, connection events, and errors. The log panel sits at the bottom of the window; open it from its header bar and drag its top edge to resize it. It can be filtered by:

- **Level** (e.g. info, warning, error)
- **Source**
- **Connection**
- **Machine**
- **Time range**

Each entry shows its time, level, source, and message. Entries about a connection or machine link to it, so you can open it in one click. Long entries expand when clicked, hovering the time shows the full timestamp (with time zone), and each line can be copied.

<Tip>
  Connections and machines have their own log panel at the bottom of their window, already filtered to that connection or machine (a machine's panel can include its connections' logs too). It's the fastest way to diagnose one thing without wading through everything else.
</Tip>

## Exporting Logs

Use **Export logs** to save logs to a file, for example to attach to a support request. Choose a format:

| Format | Best for |
|--------|----------|
| **Text** | Reading, or attaching to a support email. |
| **CSV** | Opening in Excel or another spreadsheet. |
| **JSON** | Scripts and other tools. |

The export uses the log panel's current filters, and starts from its time range. Pick a different **Time range**, or **Custom range…** for exact start and end times.

## Managing Log Storage

Logs can be cleaned up manually at any time, or cleared entirely, from the log panel. Automatic retention (how long logs are kept, and the maximum number of entries) is configured in [Settings → Logs](/gateway/settings/logs).
