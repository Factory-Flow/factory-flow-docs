The Factory Flow Gateway **Settings** screen has six sections, each documented on its own page: **Server**, **Collector**, **Connections**, **Logs**, **Application**, and **Danger Zone**.

Pick a section from the list on the left to show it. On narrower windows, the sections appear as a row of buttons above the content instead. Changes save automatically — there's no Save button.

<Tip>
  Use the **Search** box at the top of the section list to find a setting by name, or by related words (for example, "ip address", "backup", or "dark mode"). Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> on macOS or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> on Windows and Linux to jump straight to it. Use the arrow keys and <kbd>Enter</kbd> to pick a result — the app opens that section and highlights the setting.
</Tip>
