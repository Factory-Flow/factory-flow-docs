## General

Set your organization's display name and control its public visibility.

**Public Organization** — When enabled, any Factory Flow user can find your organization by name and submit a join request. When disabled, access is by invitation only. Most teams leave this off for security.

## Subscription & Usage

The **Subscription** tab shows your current plan, billing information, and renewal date.

The **Usage** tab shows how much of your plan's limits you're currently consuming:

| Resource | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Machines** | Number of machines registered vs your plan's limit. |
| **Layouts** | Number of floor layouts created. |
| **Storage** | Data storage consumed by historical analytics. |

If you're approaching a limit, you'll see a warning indicator. Contact support or upgrade your plan to increase limits.

### If your subscription ends *(Cloud only)*

When you cancel, you keep full access until the end of the billing period you've paid for. If a payment fails, you keep access while the payment is being retried.

Once the subscription has ended, members see a prompt to choose a plan, and gateways and API tokens stop working. Your data is kept, and choosing a plan brings everything back. If no plan is chosen:

| When | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| **Day 0** | The subscription ends. The organization is locked until a plan is chosen. |
| **Day 23** | Admins get an email saying the organization will be scheduled for deletion in 7 days. |
| **Day 30** | The organization is [scheduled for deletion](#danger-zone). Admins get an email and can still restore it. |
| **Day 60** | The organization and all of its data are permanently erased. |

The date the organization will be scheduled for deletion also appears on the plan selection page.

<Note>
  Self-hosted installations are never deleted because of a subscription or license. Only an Admin can delete an organization there.
</Note>

## Audit Log

The Audit Log is a chronological record of every significant action taken in your organization — who made changes, what they changed, and when.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Timestamp** | When the action occurred. |
| **User** | The member who performed the action. |
| **Action** | What was done (e.g. "Machine Created", "Member Invited", "Alert Rule Deleted"). |
| **Details** | Additional context about the specific change. |

The audit log is read-only and cannot be cleared or edited. It is available to all Admin and Editor members.

## Danger Zone

The **Danger Zone** at the bottom of Organization Settings contains the option to **Delete Organization**. Only Admins can delete an organization, and you must type the organization's name to confirm.

### What happens when you delete an organization

Organizations are also scheduled for deletion [30 days after their subscription ends](#if-your-subscription-ends-cloud-only) if no plan is chosen.

Deleting an organization doesn't erase it immediately. It is scheduled for deletion and kept for **30 days**, so a mistake can be undone:

- **Access stops right away.** The organization disappears for every member, and its gateways and API tokens stop working. No alerts are sent and no analytics are calculated while it's pending deletion.
- **Your subscription is cancelled immediately**, with no refund or credit for the rest of the billing period. *(Cloud only.)*
- **Every Admin gets an email** with the date the organization will be permanently erased.
- **After 30 days, everything is permanently erased**: machines, production and analytics history, layouts, lines, facilities, shifts, alert rules, gateways, API tokens and memberships. This can't be undone.

The organization's name stays reserved until it's permanently erased.

<Warning>
  Export any data you need before deleting. Once the 30 days have passed, the organization and its data can't be recovered.
</Warning>

### Restoring a deleted organization

Any Admin of the organization can restore it during the 30 days:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the organization picker">
    Click the link in the deletion email, or go to the organization picker after signing in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore it">
    Under **Pending deletion**, click **Restore** next to the organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resubscribe (Cloud only)">
    The subscription was cancelled when the organization was deleted, so choose a plan again to regain full access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everything comes back exactly as it was: machines, history, members, gateways and API tokens. Data your gateways tried to send while the organization was deleted isn't recorded.

### Leaving as the last member

If you're the only member of an organization, leaving it also deletes it. The same 30-day period applies, and you can restore it from the organization picker.

### Audit logs

Audit log entries are kept after an organization is permanently erased, tagged with the organization's name, so there's a record for support and billing questions.
